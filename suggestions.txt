Consistency in Tense:
Ensure consistency in verb tense. For current roles, use the present tense ("Design and develop," "manage & support"), and for past roles, use past tense ("Maintained," "Created," "Contributed").

Quantify Achievements:
Whenever possible, quantify your achievements to provide a clearer picture of your impact. For example, mentioning specific project sizes, the number of users affected, or the percentage of improvement can add depth.

Highlight Achievements:
Consider placing more emphasis on your key achievements in each role. For instance, if restructuring the backend architecture or introducing a new social login system were particularly impactful, make sure these stand out.

Organize Bullets:
Organize your bullet points consistently. For example, start each bullet point with a strong action verb and try to maintain a consistent format for ease of reading.

Separate Responsibilities and Achievements:
Make a clear distinction between your responsibilities and your achievements in each role. Responsibilities can highlight what was expected of you, while achievements showcase what you accomplished.

Use Keywords:
If possible, include relevant keywords from the job descriptions in your bullet points. This can be particularly useful if you are tailoring your resume for specific positions.

Include Technical Stack:
Consider including the technologies or programming languages you used in each role. This gives recruiters a quick overview of your technical skills.
